Domino's Gambit · Off-World Colony

A lifetime ago a terraforming mission set down in this crater to make a waypoint of it: processor stacks, a gate anchor, and a manifest that assumed resupply. The work came up crooked. Then the system gateway went — a light nobody has explained, and not one ship since — and a staging post became a home. What follows is decades of making do, seen from above.
